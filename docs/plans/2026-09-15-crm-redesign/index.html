The CRM already collects almost everything the business needs. What the audit found

A code-level read of all 17 modules, their API responses, and the shared component library. Ordered by how badly each one misleads someone running the business.

Five rules every screen follows

These are what you're approving as much as the visuals. They decide what gets cut.

Real numbers or nothingNo decorative sparklines. A chart only draws recorded values, with its period and unit written on it.
One period, comparedThe period picker controls every number on the screen, and each headline shows change vs the previous period.
Action before analysisWhat needs doing, with ₹ at stake, sits at the top. Detail comes one tap later.
Business words"Days of stock left", not "daysCover". "Number not on WhatsApp", not "#131026".
Phone is realTables become cards, boards become swipeable stages, one conversation at a time.

New navigation

Grouped by the job someone is doing, not by the integration behind it. Each hub takes one colour from the logo so you always know where you are.

How it gets built

Nothing ships until you approve the screens. Then it's built one hub at a time, and each phase gets a live test before the next starts. None of this changes what the WhatsApp bot says or when messages send. The Inbox work is visual only.

  • One token set, retire old theme
  • New shell, nav, ⌘K search
  • Shared metrics service: one revenue, one channel rule, previous period for every figure

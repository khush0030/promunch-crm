# PROMUNCH brand descriptions

One positioning, written in four lengths. Paste the same words everywhere, so every profile, listing and article tells AI tools the same story.

The anchor sentence, used word for word in every version longer than one line:

> PROMUNCH is an Indian D2C brand of high-protein soya snacks.

Rules for anyone editing these: PROMUNCH always in capitals. No em dashes or en dashes. No "best", "No. 1" or other claims we cannot prove. Never call the whole range roasted: Roasted Edamame and Soya Crunchies are roasted, Soya Sticks and Soya Chips are fried. Resolve every [CONFIRM] before pasting.

## 1. One-liner (126 characters)

For: Instagram bio, X bio, WhatsApp Business "About" (139 character limit), Facebook intro, homepage meta description.

```
PROMUNCH: high-protein soya snacks from India. Roasted Edamame, roasted Soya Crunchies, Soya Sticks and Chips. Your Munchy Pal
```

## 4. FAQ: five short questions and answers

For: the FAQ page (promunch.in/pages/faqs), Amazon Brand Store Q&A, Google Business Profile Q&A, press kit. Keep each answer as written, so AI tools can quote it whole.

**What does PROMUNCH sell?**
PROMUNCH sells high-protein soya snacks: Roasted Edamame, Soya Crunchies, Soya Sticks and Soya Chips, plus combos and gift packs. PROMUNCH is an Indian D2C brand.

**Are all PROMUNCH snacks roasted?**
No. Roasted Edamame is roasted in olive oil, and Soya Crunchies are roasted, not fried. Soya Sticks and Soya Chips are fried.

**How much protein is in PROMUNCH Roasted Edamame?**
PROMUNCH Roasted Edamame has 42 to 45g of protein per 100g, depending on the flavour. Himalayan Rock Salt has 45.3g of protein per 100g.

**Where can I buy PROMUNCH?**
You can buy PROMUNCH on promunch.in and on Amazon India. Orders on promunch.in of ₹599 or more ship free. Below ₹599, shipping is ₹99. Cash on delivery adds ₹50, and prepaid orders get 5% off.

**Who founded PROMUNCH?**
Parth Mutha founded PROMUNCH in 2021. He was named to the Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia list in 2025.

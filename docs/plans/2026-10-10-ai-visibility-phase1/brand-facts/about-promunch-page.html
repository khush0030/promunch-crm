What we sell

PROMUNCH makes savoury, crunchy snacks with real protein. We are clear about how each one is made. Our Roasted Edamame is roasted in olive oil. Our Soya Crunchies are roasted, not fried. Our Soya Sticks and Soya Chips are fried. Not every PROMUNCH snack is roasted.

Founder story

PROMUNCH was founded in 2021 by Parth Mutha.

Parth saw that most savoury snacks in India were fried namkeen and chips with very little protein. He wanted a crunchy snack that actually fills you up. That is why he started PROMUNCH.

In 2025, Parth was named to the Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia list.

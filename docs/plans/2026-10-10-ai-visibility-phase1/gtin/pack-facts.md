# Pack facts transcribed from the 28 pack photos (10 Oct 2026)

Source: 28 phone photos of PROMUNCH pack **backs** (no fronts were photographed). Photo files are named by UUID; the short id (first 8 characters) is used below. Transcriptions copy the pack text exactly, including its spelling and punctuation. `[UNREADABLE]` = present but not legible; `[NOT IN PHOTO]` = cropped out or on a panel that was not photographed. Do not fill these from memory.

GTIN list and how each was checked: [gtins.csv](gtins.csv).

## What the photos can and cannot settle

| Question | Answer from the packs |
|---|---|
| Roasted / fried / baked wording | **None of the photographed back panels says roasted, fried or baked.** The only cooking-oil evidence: Edamame ingredients end in "Olive Oil"; the Sticks Chatpata Masala ingredient fragment ends in "...il (Ricebran)". Fronts (where "Roasted" / "Made with Ricebran Oil" usually appear) were not photographed. |
| Veg mark (green dot in square) | **Not seen on any photo.** The only green dot (14cc8cab, Sticks Cream & Onion) is a plain dot with no square outline, so it is probably decorative. The FSSAI veg mark is usually on the front, which was not photographed. |
| Vegan / gluten-free / no palm oil / no maida / non-GMO / no preservatives | **No such claim appears on any photographed panel.** Do not publish them on the strength of these photos. |
| Shelf life | Only one pack has ink-jet dates: 6bb7c425 (150g, probably Edamame Indori Chatka): DATE OF MFG. 07/10/2026, USE BY 06/04/2027, BATCH NO. 0710 = **6 months**. All other packs have blank MFG/USE BY/BATCH fields. c53cca8e shows a faint set-off ghost of dates in the label box: [UNREADABLE]. |
| Storage | "NOT TO BE SOLD LOOSE / Store in a cool and dry place, keep away from direct sunlight." (all three lines; wording varies by line breaks only) |
| Allergens | Crunchies: "Allergen Information: Contains Soya". Edamame: "ALLERGEN ADVICE : CONTAINS SOY". Sticks Chatpata Masala: "...oduct is made in a facility that ... Milk, Wheat, Sesame, Mustard" (left part cropped: [NOT IN PHOTO]). |
| Who makes it | Crunchies: made by VIPPY (Dewas). Sticks/Chips: made by Aggarwal Food Products, Delhi; VIPPY "Repacked & Marketed". Edamame: VIPPY "Imported, Manufactured & Marketed", Country of Origin People's Republic of China. |
| Pack-size conflicts with the repo | Noodle Masala Crunchies large pack label says **270g** (₹260), not 300g. Sticks/Chips packs say **100g** (Shopify titles mention 80g in places). |

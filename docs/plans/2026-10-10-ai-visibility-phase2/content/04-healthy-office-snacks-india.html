The ideas, one by one

1. Roasted chana

The classic Indian desk snack. Roasted chana has about 22g of protein per 100g, so a 30g handful gives about 6.6g. It is cheap, keeps for weeks in a jar, and does not leave greasy fingers on your keyboard. Add a pinch of chaat masala if plain chana feels boring.

2. Dry roasted peanuts

Peanuts have about 24g of protein per 100g, so 30g gives about 7g. They are also high in fat, so portion them into a small box rather than eating from a big packet. Dry roasted is a better pick than fried if you want less added oil.

3. Almonds

Almonds have about 21g of protein per 100g. A 30g portion, roughly 25 almonds, gives about 6.4g. They cost more than peanuts, so they are less protein per rupee, but they keep well and are easy to share.

4. Boiled eggs

A large egg has about 6.3g of protein, so two eggs give about 12.6g. Boil them at home, peel them, and carry them in a small box. Eat them within a few hours or keep them in the office fridge.

5. Chana chaat

Boiled chickpeas with onion, tomato, lemon and masala. One cup of boiled chickpeas (about 164g) has about 14.5g of protein. Pack the chickpeas and the wet toppings separately so it stays fresh until lunch or the 4 pm slump.

6. Greek yogurt

Plain Greek yogurt has about 10g of protein per 100g, so a 150g cup gives about 15g. Regular dahi has much less protein, because Greek yogurt is strained to remove water. Check the label, as protein varies by brand. Needs a fridge.

7. Dry roasted soya nuts

Dry roasted soybeans have about 43g of protein per 100g, which is very high for a plant food. A 30g handful gives about 13g. They are crunchy, so they work well as a replacement for fried namkeen.

8. Makhana

Makhana is light and crunchy. Because it is so light, even a big bowl may weigh only about 25g. Published analyses put it at roughly 8g to 12g of protein per 100g, so 25g gives about 2g to 3g. Enjoy it for the crunch, but pair it with something else if you want more protein, for example a few peanuts.

9. Roasted edamame

Edamame is young soybean. Once it is roasted and dried, its protein per 100g is high. PROMUNCH Roasted Edamame is roasted in olive oil and has 42g to 45g of protein per 100g, depending on the flavour. A 25g pack works out to about 10.5g to 11.3g of protein. Small packs make portions simple: one pack, one snack. The Edamame Travel Combo has 9 packs of 25g, one for each of nine office days.

10. Protein shake

If you already use protein powder, a shaker bottle at your desk is the fastest option. Protein per scoop depends on the powder, so read the label. As a guide, pure whey protein concentrate (WPC 80) is about 80% protein, so a 30g scoop has about 24g. Flavoured blends usually have less.

Tips for keeping office snacks easy

  • Portion on Sunday. Fill five small boxes or zip bags with chana, peanuts or soya nuts for the week. Eating from a big packet makes it hard to keep track.
  • Mix a light snack with a protein snack. Makhana with peanuts, or fruit with a handful of chana, gives you crunch and protein.
  • Choose low mess. Dry snacks in small packs or boxes are the easiest at a desk or in meetings.
  • Read labels for packaged snacks. Compare protein per 100g, check whether the snack is roasted or fried, and look at salt.
  • Think about smell. Your teammates will thank you for skipping the egg and onion chaat in a closed meeting room.

Stocking an office pantry

If you manage snacks for a team, a mix works best: one budget staple (roasted chana or peanuts), one light option (makhana or fruit), and one ready-to-eat packaged protein snack in single portions. Frequently asked questions

What is a good high-protein office snack in India?

Easy options include roasted chana (about 6.6g of protein per 30g), peanuts (about 7g per 30g), dry roasted soya nuts (about 13g per 30g), boiled eggs (about 6.3g each) and roasted edamame snacks. PROMUNCH Roasted Edamame gives about 10.5g to 11.3g of protein per 25g pack.

Which office snacks do not need a fridge?

Roasted chana, peanuts, almonds, makhana, dry roasted soya nuts and sealed packaged snacks like roasted edamame keep at room temperature. Eggs, chaat and yogurt are best kept cool.

How much protein is in makhana?

Published analyses put makhana at roughly 8g to 12g of protein per 100g. A typical 25g bowl has about 2g to 3g of protein.

## FAQ schema (suggested Q&As)

1. **What is a good high-protein office snack in India?** Easy options include roasted chana (about 6.6g of protein per 30g), peanuts (about 7g per 30g), dry roasted soya nuts (about 13g per 30g), boiled eggs (about 6.3g each) and roasted edamame snacks. PROMUNCH Roasted Edamame gives about 10.5g to 11.3g of protein per 25g pack.
2. **Which office snacks do not need a fridge?** Roasted chana, peanuts, almonds, makhana, dry roasted soya nuts and sealed packaged snacks like roasted edamame keep at room temperature. Eggs, chaat and yogurt are best kept cool.
3. **How much protein is in makhana?** Published analyses put makhana at roughly 8g to 12g of protein per 100g. A typical 25g bowl has about 2g to 3g of protein.
4. **Does PROMUNCH supply office pantries?** Yes. PROMUNCH supplies office pantries and takes bulk and corporate orders through the bulk order form at promunch.in/pages/bulk-orders.

## FAQ schema (suggested Q&As)

Use only if the theme supports Article FAQ JSON-LD (via a metafield or a FAQ app). The answers match the visible FAQ section word for word. The body HTML has no script tags, as required.

1. **Which snack has the most protein per 100g in India under ₹499?** Among common snack types, roasted soya and roasted edamame are usually the highest. Plain dry roasted soybeans have about 43g of protein per 100g. Roasted chana and peanuts follow at about 22g to 24g per 100g. Packaged snacks vary, so always check the label.
2. **Is makhana a good source of protein?** Makhana has some protein, roughly 8g to 12g per 100g in published analyses. That is less than roasted chana, peanuts or soya snacks. It is a good light snack, but not a high-protein one.
3. **How do I compare protein value between two snacks?** Multiply protein per 100g by the pack weight and divide by 100 to get total protein. Divide that by the price and multiply by 100 to get protein per ₹100. Add shipping if you buy online.
4. **How much protein is in PROMUNCH Roasted Edamame?** PROMUNCH Roasted Edamame has 42g to 45g of protein per 100g, depending on the flavour. Himalayan Rock Salt has 45.3g per 100g.

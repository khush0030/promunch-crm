Short answer: gram for gram, whey protein powder has the most protein, because it is a concentrated powder. Among whole foods, soya is well ahead of chickpea: dry roasted soybeans have about 43g of protein per 100g, while roasted chana has about 22g. Whey and soya also score higher than chickpea on protein quality. But the right choice also depends on your diet, budget and what you will actually eat.

First, compare like with like

Protein numbers change a lot depending on the form of the food. Cooking in water adds weight, so boiled chickpeas have less protein per 100g than dry or roasted chickpeas. Roasting removes water, so roasted foods have more protein per 100g than fresh ones. A powder like whey is mostly protein by design. Always check whether a number is for dry, cooked, roasted or powdered food.

Protein quality: not all grams are equal

Your body builds protein from amino acids. Nine of them are "essential", which means food has to supply them. Scientists score protein quality with two systems:

  • PDCAAS (Protein Digestibility Corrected Amino Acid Score), the older score, capped at 1.00.
  • DIAAS (Digestible Indispensable Amino Acid Score), recommended by the FAO in 2013. It can go above 1.00.

Higher is better on both. Here are published values:

Protein PDCAAS DIAAS
Whey protein isolate 1.00 1.09
Soy protein isolate 0.98 0.90
Chickpeas 0.52 0.67

What this means in plain words:

  • Whey is a very high quality protein. It comes from milk.
  • Soy protein is close behind. It is one of the few plant proteins that scores near the top.
  • Chickpea protein scores lower on its own, mainly because it is low in the amino acid methionine. Cereals like wheat and rice have more methionine, which is one reason dal with roti or rice works well together.

These scores come from lab tests on isolated proteins and specific foods, so treat them as a guide, not an exact measure of what your body gets from one snack.

How much protein do you need in a day?

ICMR-NIN (2020) sets the Recommended Dietary Allowance for healthy Indian adults at 0.83g of protein per kg of body weight per day. For a 60kg adult that is about 50g a day. Needs differ with age, activity, pregnancy and health, so speak to a doctor or dietitian for advice about you.

So which should you choose?

  • Most protein per spoon: whey powder, if you eat dairy and like shakes.
  • Most protein from a whole plant food: soya. Dry roasted soybeans and roasted edamame snacks are ready to eat.
  • Lowest cost and very easy to find: chickpeas, as roasted chana, dal or chaat. Pair with cereals for better protein quality.
  • Simplest plan: mix them. Variety across the day matters more than any single food.

Frequently asked questions

Which has more protein, soya or chickpea?

Soya. Dry roasted soybeans have about 43g of protein per 100g, while roasted chana has about 22g. Soy protein also scores higher than chickpea protein on quality scores like PDCAAS and DIAAS.

Is soy protein as good as whey?

Whey scores slightly higher on protein quality (DIAAS 1.09 for whey isolate against 0.90 for soy isolate in published tests), but soy is one of the highest scoring plant proteins. For vegetarians who avoid dairy, soya is a strong option.

Why do boiled chickpeas have less protein than roasted chana?

Boiled chickpeas absorb water, which adds weight without adding protein. Roasting removes water. So 100g of boiled chickpeas has about 8.9g of protein, while 100g of roasted chana has about 22g.

How much protein does an Indian adult need per day?

ICMR-NIN (2020) recommends 0.83g of protein per kg of body weight per day for healthy adults. That is about 50g a day for a 60kg adult.

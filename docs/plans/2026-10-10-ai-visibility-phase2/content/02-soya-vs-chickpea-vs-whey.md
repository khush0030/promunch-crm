## FAQ schema (suggested Q&As)

Same text as the visible FAQ section.

1. **Which has more protein, soya or chickpea?** Soya. Dry roasted soybeans have about 43g of protein per 100g, while roasted chana has about 22g. Soy protein also scores higher than chickpea protein on quality scores like PDCAAS and DIAAS.
2. **Is soy protein as good as whey?** Whey scores slightly higher on protein quality (DIAAS 1.09 for whey isolate against 0.90 for soy isolate in published tests), but soy is one of the highest scoring plant proteins. For vegetarians who avoid dairy, soya is a strong option.
3. **Why do boiled chickpeas have less protein than roasted chana?** Boiled chickpeas absorb water, which adds weight without adding protein. Roasting removes water. So 100g of boiled chickpeas has about 8.9g of protein, while 100g of roasted chana has about 22g.
4. **How much protein does an Indian adult need per day?** ICMR-NIN (2020) recommends 0.83g of protein per kg of body weight per day for healthy adults. That is about 50g a day for a 60kg adult.

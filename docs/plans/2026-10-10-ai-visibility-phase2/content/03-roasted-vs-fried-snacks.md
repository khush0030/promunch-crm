## FAQ schema (suggested Q&As)

1. **Does roasting reduce protein?** Not in any meaningful way. USDA data for almonds shows about 21g of protein per 100g whether they are raw, dry roasted or oil roasted. Roasting removes water, so protein per 100g can even rise slightly.
2. **Do roasted snacks have no oil?** Not always. Dry roasting uses no oil, but many roasted snacks are roasted with a little oil. Check the ingredients list.
3. **Are all PROMUNCH snacks roasted?** No. PROMUNCH Roasted Edamame is roasted in olive oil, and Soya Crunchies are roasted, not fried. Soya Sticks and Soya Chips are fried.
4. **Why are fried chips so much higher in fat than potatoes?** Raw potato has less than 0.1g of fat per 100g. Frying replaces water in the potato slices with oil, so plain salted potato chips end up with about 34g of fat per 100g.

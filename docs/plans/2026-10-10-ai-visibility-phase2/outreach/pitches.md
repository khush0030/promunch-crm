# PROMUNCH outreach pitches

Written 10 Oct 2026. Nothing here has been sent. Every email goes out from Parth Mutha's own mailbox, **parth@trypromunch.in**, with reply-to the same address and Parth's sign-off. Send each one by hand, one recipient per email (no BCC blasts).

## Before you send any of these

1. Fill every `{curly}` slot with something specific to the person. A pitch that could go to anyone gets ignored.
2. Resolve or delete every `[CONFIRM: ...]`. Never send a pitch with a bracket still in it.
3. Copy check: PROMUNCH in capitals; no em dashes or en dashes; no "best", "No. 1", "healthiest" or similar about our own products; no claim that is not in the fact list below; never mention the old agency name.
4. Roasted vs fried, every time: Roasted Edamame and Soya Crunchies are roasted. Soya Sticks and Soya Chips are fried. Never call the whole range roasted, baked or "not fried".
5. Each body below is 150 words or fewer, sign-off included. Keep it that way after you personalise.

Rules

  1. One red thing per screen: the primary button or the number that matters.
  2. Every page opens with a title, one plain sentence and at most 4 numbers.
  3. Details live one click deeper, in a drawer or detail page.
  4. No more than 5 tabs, 5 table columns, 3 KPI tiles on a phone row of 2.
  5. Status = word + colour, never colour alone.
  6. PROMUNCH in caps, no em dashes, "Your Munchy Pal" as the sign-off.
  7. Body text never below 14px; mono labels never below 11px.

Rules every screen follows
  1. A title, one plain sentence, at most 4 numbers.
  2. One red thing per screen.
  3. Details one click deeper, in a drawer.
  4. No more than 5 tabs or 5 table columns.
  5. Status is a word plus a colour, never colour alone.
  6. Body text 16px, nothing under 14px.

73 screens and 60 pop-ups, each at laptop and phone width. All names, numbers and orders are illustrative; product names and facts are real PROMUNCH ones.

Why this order: the top three are what the team opens every morning (what's waiting, who's talking, what must ship). Grow is where marketing work happens. Know is where you look things up. Settings is rarely used, so it sits in the footer.

Why Marketing has two sub-items instead of two top-level entries: it keeps the sidebar at 8 and makes "where do I send a message to many people?" one answer. The two sub-items only appear when Marketing is open.

**Badges** only count things a human must act on (never totals): Inbox = tickets + chats needing a human; Orders = unconfirmed COD; Creators = items "waiting on us". Badges are grey; on the active item they turn red.

**Access**: each item maps 1:1 to an access area in `src/lib/access.ts` (Home & Maya · Inbox · Orders · Marketing · Creators · B2B · Customers · Insights · Settings). Locked items are hidden, never greyed. Today's `bot_knowledge` and `audience` areas fold into Inbox and Customers; `whatsapp` + `email` areas become sub-areas of Marketing.

## Laptop behaviour
- 248px light sidebar. "Collapse" turns it into a 76px icon rail with red dots for badges (auto below 1024px).
- Top bar: one wide search ("Search orders, customers, tickets, anything", ⌘K) and a notification bell. No other global buttons.
- Page header, same on every page: mono eyebrow (where you are) → UPPERCASE title → one plain sentence with the key number in bold → right side: period picker and **one** primary button; anything else goes in a "⋯" menu.
- Tabs sit under the header, max 5. Details open in a right drawer (560 to 760px) so you never lose your list.

## Phone behaviour (≤ 760px)
- Top: logo, search, bell.
- Bottom tab bar: **Home · Inbox · (Maya) · Orders · More**. Maya is the raised yellow centre button.
- **More** opens a sheet with WhatsApp, Email, Creators, B2B & deals, Customers, Insights, Settings, each with a one-line description.
- Tables become labelled cards, boards swipe one column at a time, drawers and modals become bottom sheets, the inbox becomes list → conversation with a back arrow.
- One primary button is full-width in the header.

## Global search / command palette (⌘K)
One box, four groups of results: **Jump to** (pages and actions like "New WhatsApp campaign", "Add a creator collab"), **Customers** (name, phone, email, city), **Orders** (#PM number, Amazon id), **Tickets** (#number, subject). Last line always offers "Ask Maya: <your text>". Keyboard: ↑↓ Enter, Esc closes. On phones it opens full screen.

## Notifications
Bell drawer lists only events worth interrupting for: SLA breach, voice-agent results, creator drafts in, campaign finished, stock running out. Each person chooses sound, WhatsApp ping and digests in Settings → My notifications. The existing ops escalations ("done #N") stay on WhatsApp.

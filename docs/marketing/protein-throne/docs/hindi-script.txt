Reading this script
The dialogue is in conversational Hinglish, the way people actually talk on Reels. English
words like protein, crunch, snack and check stay in English on purpose. Each line has
Devanagari (for Hindi TTS / voice artists) and Roman script (for captions and the team).


   Character                                          Voice direction

   Narrator                                           Over-serious, mythological-TV-serial style. Deep,
                                                      slow, dramatic pauses.

   Makhana                                            Posh and royal. Nasal, delicate, over-polite, easily
                                                      offended.

   Peanut                                             Desi pehelwan. Loud, proud, "bhai" in every line.

   Protein Chips                                      Filmy superhero. Hero-entry energy, over-the-top
                                                      announcements.

   Protein Bar                                        Gym bro. Deep, clipped, talks like a fitness coach.

   PROMUNCH Edamame                                   Calm, deep baritone, deadpan. Never raises his
                                                      voice. Every line lands like a mic drop.

   Crown                                              Echoing, regal, slightly bored.




Episode 1: पुराने राजा (The Old Rulers)
Scene: Snacks की सल्तनत. A golden crown floats above the empty Protein Throne.

NARRATOR: बहुत समय पहले... Snacks की सल्तनत में... एक ताज था। और एक सिंहासन। Protein का सिंहासन।
Bahut samay pehle... Snacks ki Saltanat mein... ek taaj tha. Aur ek singhaasan. Protein ka
Singhaasan.

MAKHANA (glides in, nose up): हल्का, शाही और sophisticated. ताज तो ज़ाहिर है... मेरा ही है। Halka,
shaahi aur sophisticated. Taaj toh zaahir hai... mera hi hai.

PEANUT (slaps his thighs, akhada entry): अरे भाई! जब protein cool भी नहीं था, तब से हम protein
बाँट रहे हैं! Arre bhai! Jab protein cool bhi nahi tha, tab se hum protein baant rahe hain!

PROTEIN CHIPS (lands in hero pose, cape flapping): Hello? मेरे तो नाम में ही protein है! Hello?
Mere toh naam mein hi protein hai!

PROTEIN BAR (flexes, stone-faced): तुम सब snacks हो। मैं... performance nutrition हूँ। Tum sab
snacks ho. Main... performance nutrition hoon.

NARRATOR: और फिर... वही हुआ जो हर खानदान में होता है। Aur phir... wahi hua jo har khaandaan

(All four lunge for the crown. It flashes and blasts them across the palace. Crumbs
everywhere.)

CROWN: ताज छीना नहीं जाता। कमाया जाता है। चार परीक्षाएँ। Taaj chheena nahi jaata. Kamaaya jaata
hai. Chaar pareekshaayein.

ON-SCREEN: PROTEIN · CRUNCH · स्वाद (TASTE) · रोज़ की SNACKABILITY

(The palace doors shake. Silence. A tiny green bean rolls in. Everyone bursts out laughing.)

PEANUT: ये? ये आया है ताज लेने? Ye? Ye aaya hai taaj lene?

MAKHANA: यही है challenger? इसे तो कोई चटनी में पीस देगा। Yahi hai challenger? Ise toh koi
chutney mein pees dega.

(The bean's shadow grows into a massive armoured warrior on the wall. Laughter stops.)

EDAMAME (calm): हँस लो। अभी टाइम है। Hans lo. Abhi time hai.

END CARD: Episode 2 — महा स्नैक दंगल (The Great Snack Smackdown)


Episode 2: महा स्नैक दंगल (The Great Snack Smackdown)
(The tiny bean transforms into the PROMUNCH Edamame warrior. Red-and-gold armour
locks into place.)

EDAMAME: पहले कौन आएगा? Pehle kaun aayega?


Makhana fight
MAKHANA (charging, slow motion): शाही... हमलाआआआ! Shaahi... hamlaaaaa!

(One effortless slap. Makhana ricochets off three pillars and lands in a bowl. Silent beat.)

EDAMAME: हल्का तो है... वाक़ई। Halka toh hai... waaqai.


Peanut fight
PEANUT (leaping off the top rope): मूँगफली... पावर!! Moongphali... POWER!!

(Edamame catches him mid-air with one hand.)

PEANUT (tiny voice): भाई... बैठ के बात करते हैं? Bhai... baith ke baat karte hain?

(Theatrical wrestling spin, then SLAM into a giant bowl of masala. Seasoning cloud.)

EDAMAME: अब आया ना स्वाद। Ab aaya na swaad.


Protein Bar fight
PROTEIN BAR: Discipline को कोई नहीं तोड़ सकता। Discipline ko koi nahi tod sakta.

(Edamame bends him into a horseshoe, then squeezes him into a tiny cube.)

PROTEIN BAR (squeaky): मैं इस shape में काम नहीं कर सकता। Main is shape mein kaam nahi kar
sakta.

(Flick. Cube flies off-screen. Distant "ting".)


Protein Chips fight
(Protein Chips zooms around firing protein-shaped energy beams.)

PROTEIN CHIPS: मेरी छाती पर PROTEIN लिखा है!! Meri chhaati par PROTEIN likha hai!!

EDAMAME: लिखना आसान है। Likhna aasaan hai.

(Catches the pouch, shakes it fast, one finger tap. It collapses into a harmless pile of
crumbs.)

EDAMAME: निभाना मुश्किल। Nibhaana mushkil.


The crown refuses
(Edamame walks to the crown. It floats up, out of reach.)

NARRATOR: लड़ाई जीतना काफ़ी नहीं था। राजा बनने के लिए... उसे सब कु छ साबित करना था। Ladaai jeetna
kaafi nahi tha. Raja banne ke liye... use sab kuch saabit karna tha.

EDAMAME (cracks neck): चलो। Warm-up ख़त्म। Chalo. Warm-up khatam.

shortcut nahi.

Test 2: Crunch
(A royal judge bites a PROMUNCH Edamame. Slow motion, then a massive CRUNCH
shockwave. Palace bells ring. Makhana's moustache flies off.)

JUDGE: Crunch... confirm है। Crunch... confirm hai.

MAKHANA (patting his bare lip): किसी ने मेरी मूँछ देखी है? Kisi ne meri moonchh dekhi hai?


Test 3: Taste
(The three flavours rise as elemental warriors, one line each.)

HIMALAYAN ROCK SALT (snow and salt crystals): ठं डा दिमाग़, पक्का स्वाद। Thanda dimaag,
pakka swaad.

MASALA MANIA (fire and spice): तीखा हूँ... संभाल पाओगे? Teekha hoon... sambhaal paaoge?

INDORI CHATKA (colourful Indori street style): भिया, चटका तो लगेगा! Bhiya, chatka toh lagega!

EDAMAME: Protein यहाँ लाया। स्वाद ने राजा बनाया। Protein yahaan laaya. Swaad ne raja banaaya.


Test 4: Everyday snackability
(Fast cuts: office desk → gym bag → airport lounge → hotel minibar → road trip → movie
night with friends.)

NARRATOR: Meeting से workout तक। Flight से movie night तक। एक protein snack... जिसे खाने
का सच में मन करे। Meeting se workout tak. Flight se movie night tak. Ek protein snack... jise
khaane ka sach mein mann kare.


Coronation
CROWN: Protein? EDAMAME: Check.

CROWN: Crunch? EDAMAME: ज़ाहिर है। Zaahir hai.

CROWN: स्वाद? Swaad?

(Cut to Peanut in the corner, secretly munching from a PROMUNCH pouch. Caught.)

PEANUT (mouth full, sighs): दिल पे पत्थर रख के ... check। Dil pe patthar rakh ke... check.

(The crown descends onto the PROMUNCH pack. All four former rulers kneel.)

CROWD: Protein Snacks के राजा की... जय हो! Protein Snacks ke Raja ki... jai ho!

Production notes
Claim check: "100g में 45g तक PROTEIN" and "King of Protein Snacks" must be matched to
the approved labels for each flavour before lock. A superiority claim like "King" may need
substantiation under ASCI comparative-advertising rules, so keep all rival snacks generic
and unbranded.

Timing: Hindi lines run about 10–15% longer than English. Episode 3 is the tightest; if it
runs past 50s, trim the three flavour-warrior lines first.
